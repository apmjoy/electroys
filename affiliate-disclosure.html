Affiliate Disclosure

A clear explanation of how referral commissions and editorial independence work.

Effective date: September 28, 2026

Plain-language disclosure: Electroys may earn a commission when you buy through certain qualifying links. If a link is affiliate-enabled, the retailer may pay Electroys a referral fee. Your price is not increased because of that referral.

How affiliate links work

Electroys may participate in affiliate or referral programs offered by retailers, manufacturers, affiliate networks, and marketplaces. Not every outbound product link is necessarily an affiliate link. Where a commercial relationship applies, Electroys aims to disclose it clearly near the recommendation or link.

Editorial independence

Affiliate eligibility does not determine whether a product is included in an Electroys guide. Reviews and comparison pages are designed around product fit, learning value, age or skill considerations, compatibility, setup, and practical buying factors. When content is based on manufacturer documentation rather than hands-on testing, we say so.

Prices, availability, and seller information

Prices, discounts, stock, bundle contents, shipping, seller identity, and return terms can change after an Electroys page is published. Always confirm the final details on the retailer or manufacturer's website before purchasing.

Sponsored placements and free products

If Electroys later accepts sponsored content, paid placements, or free products for evaluation, those relationships will be disclosed on the relevant page. A sponsored relationship will not be presented as an independent hands-on review.

Program-specific disclosures

Some affiliate programs require specific wording or additional notices. Electroys will add those disclosures when and where they apply.
