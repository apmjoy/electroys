Privacy Policy

How Electroys collects, uses, and protects information.

Effective date: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Electroys handles information when you use electroys.com, subscribe to updates, contact us, or follow links to third-party websites.

Information we collect

Newsletter information. If you subscribe, we collect the name and email address you submit, along with subscription status, timestamps, source information, and technical confirmation data needed to manage double opt-in and unsubscribe requests.

Messages you send us. If you contact Electroys by email, we receive the information you include in your message.

Basic technical information. Hosting, email, and infrastructure providers may process routine technical information such as IP addresses, browser information, request logs, and security data as part of operating their services.

How we use information

  • Send updates you requested and manage subscription confirmations or unsubscribes.
  • Respond to questions, corrections, privacy requests, and business inquiries.
  • Operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the website.
  • Maintain records reasonably necessary for compliance and abuse prevention.

Service providers

Electroys currently uses GitHub Pages for website hosting and Google services, including Apps Script, Sheets, and email functionality, for newsletter operations. The site may also load fonts, libraries, images, or other resources from third-party content providers.

Retailer and manufacturer links

Electroys links to independent retailers, manufacturers, and other websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. When you leave Electroys, review the destination site's privacy information before providing personal data.

Cookies and browser storage

Electroys may use browser storage or similar technology for interface functionality. Third-party services or websites you visit from Electroys may use their own cookies or tracking technologies. If advertising, analytics, or additional tracking tools are added later, this policy will be updated to describe them.

Children's privacy

Electroys publishes STEM content that families may use together, but the newsletter subscription is intended for adults. We do not knowingly ask children to submit personal information through the newsletter form.

Data retention and your choices

Subscriber information is retained while a subscription is active and for a reasonable period afterward when needed for suppression, security, recordkeeping, or legal purposes. Every marketing email is intended to include an unsubscribe option. You may also request access, correction, or deletion by contacting us.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to a small informational website. No internet or email system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Electroys adds services, partners, or features. The effective date at the top will be revised when material changes are published.

Privacy questions

Email myelectroys@gmail.com for privacy or subscription questions.
