Privacy Policy

How Electroys collects, uses, and protects information.

Effective date: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Electroys handles information when you visit electroys.com, subscribe to updates, contact us, or follow links to other websites.

Information we collect and why

Newsletter information. We collect the name and email address you submit, together with subscription status, timestamps, signup source and confirmation or unsubscribe records. We use this information to manage subscription requests, send confirmation messages and requested updates, and process unsubscribes.

Messages. When you email Electroys, we receive the information you include and use it to respond to your inquiry. Please do not send sensitive personal information through general inquiries.

Usage and technical information. Analytics, hosting and security services may process page visits, referring pages, interaction events, cookie identifiers, IP addresses, browser and device information, and request or security logs. We use these services to understand website use, improve content, operate the site and prevent abuse.

Google Analytics

Electroys uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors find and use the website. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar identifiers to measure visits and interactions. Depending on the service settings, information may include pages viewed, referral sources, engagement events, browser and device characteristics, and approximate geographic location. Google receives technical information, including your IP address, when your browser connects to its services. Google's processing and privacy controls can vary by region and service settings.

Our newsletter name and email fields are not configured as Google Analytics event data. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Sheets, Apps Script and email

We use Google Apps Script to process newsletter requests and Google Sheets to store and manage subscriber records. These records include the signup information and subscription-management data described above. Google services also support confirmation and newsletter email delivery. Submitting a subscription request sends that information to Google's services for processing on behalf of Electroys. These services are separate from Google Analytics and serve the newsletter function. Google's handling of information is described in its Privacy Policy.

Cloudflare Turnstile

We use Cloudflare Turnstile on the subscription form to help distinguish people from automated traffic and reduce spam. Cloudflare processes security signals such as IP address, browser or user-agent information, connection characteristics, and the website associated with the verification. A verification token is used in the subscription process. Cloudflare also uses signals to improve its bot detection. Turnstile is a security service, not our audience analytics tool.

See the Cloudflare Turnstile Privacy Addendum and Cloudflare Privacy Policy. Blocking Turnstile may prevent you from completing a subscription request.

Hosting and externally loaded resources

Electroys uses GitHub Pages for hosting. Fonts, software libraries, product images and other resources may load from Google or other third-party servers. Loading those resources connects your browser to the provider, which may receive your IP address, browser information and the requested resource. Providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate.

Retailers, brands and affiliate links

Electroys links to independent retailers, manufacturers and marketplaces. Some links are affiliate links. Clicking them may allow the destination retailer or affiliate network to use cookies, referral identifiers or similar technology to attribute a visit or purchase. Their own privacy policies govern their services. See our Affiliate Disclosure for commercial relationships.

A brand name or logo on Electroys does not by itself mean sponsorship or an approved affiliate relationship. Our selection of brands may change. We update privacy disclosures when a change introduces different data collection, tracking or service providers.

Cookies and your choices

Google Analytics may set first-party analytics cookies. Other service providers and linked websites may use their own cookies or similar technologies. You can use your browser's controls to block or delete cookies; some functions may be affected. Blocking third-party cookies alone may not stop first-party analytics cookies.

Google provides an Analytics opt-out browser add-on for supported browsers. This addresses Google Analytics collection, not all website or provider processing. Unsubscribing from our newsletter does not separately disable website analytics.

Retention and privacy requests

We retain subscriber information while a subscription is active and afterward as reasonably needed for unsubscribe suppression, security, recordkeeping or legal obligations. Analytics and provider logs follow the applicable service settings and provider retention practices. You can unsubscribe using the link in our emails or contact us. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your information or object to certain processing. Send requests to myelectroys@gmail.com; we may need to verify your request.

Children's privacy

Electroys publishes STEM content families may use together, but newsletter subscriptions are intended for adults. We do not knowingly seek newsletter information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us so we can review and address it.

Security and policy changes

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information we handle, but no internet or email system is completely secure. We may update this policy as our services and practices change and will revise the effective date when material changes are published.

Privacy questions

Contact myelectroys@gmail.com for privacy or subscription questions.
