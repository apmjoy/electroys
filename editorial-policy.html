Editorial Policy

How Electroys researches, reviews, updates, and corrects product information.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Electroys is a curated STEM and electronics discovery site. This editorial policy explains how we research products, write reviews, correct errors, and separate editorial judgment from affiliate relationships.

How products are researched

We start with primary sources whenever practical: manufacturer product pages, manuals, support documentation, official education resources, and retailer listings for current availability. We may use additional reputable sources to clarify compatibility or market context.

Hands-on testing vs. documentation-based reviews

Electroys does not imply hands-on testing when it has not occurred. A page based on product documentation is labeled accordingly. If a product is physically tested later, the page will state what was tested, for how long, and any important limitations.

What we evaluate

  • What the learner can build, code, explore, or understand.
  • Age guidance, skill level, and likely adult-help requirements.
  • Setup, software, device, power, and subscription requirements.
  • Room to grow beyond the first activity.
  • Practical drawbacks, limitations, and purchase considerations.
  • Current price context and availability when it materially affects the recommendation.

Affiliate relationships

Affiliate commissions may support Electroys. Program availability may influence which products or retailers we feature or retain, but commissions do not guarantee favorable coverage. Some listings may use ordinary links without an affiliate relationship. Our evaluations should reflect the evidence, learning value and practical limitations described on each page.

Corrections

If a material product fact is wrong or outdated, we aim to correct the page and update its review date. Readers and manufacturers may send factual corrections to myelectroys@gmail.com. Requests for favorable treatment are not treated as factual corrections.

Deals

Deal pages and deal cards should identify when a price was checked. Because prices change quickly, visitors should verify the final retailer price before purchasing.
